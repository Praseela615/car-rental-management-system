ABOUT DRIVE EASE

More than a rental. It's freedom.

DriveEase is a student-built car rental management system designed around the real-world rental journey: discover a vehicle, compare options, choose dates, validate customer details and confirm a booking.

The project demonstrates responsive UI design, JavaScript search and filtering, form validation, dynamic rendering and LocalStorage-based booking history.
